## Controls
The controls are rather simple. You move the chopper using either whatever kind of circle pad your phone has, or the numbers 8,4,6,2 on the keypad for each cardinal direction. When locking onto things, you can either press the Fire button (the button in the middle of the circle pad) or 5 on the keypad to toggle the target.

Moving the chopper works like maneuvering a real helicopter in the air - it will turn when moving in another direction and change the angle of the range it can lock onto targets. If you move in a direction and then stop, it will glide a bit in the air before stopping which can be useful for positioning your target range onto turrets.

Pressing the right menu button on your phone will open the map, which will also pause the game. Here you can see the entire level and where you are in it. Pressing the right button will return you to the game while the left button will abort the mission and return you to the main menu.

The left menu button will attempt to place an artillery strike where you are. By default you have no artillery strikes and will be labelled as "Art0", but as you acquire artilleries it will change to "Art1", "Art2" and so on. Pressing the top-right button twice (to open the map and then close it) will show this information in the bottom menu of the screen.

## Mission #00 - Fast attack
This is the first level in the game and it's quite simple. It gives you plenty of space to get a feel for the controls and a single turret to lock onto and shoot at. Just make your way to the right side of the level and as you get closer the turret will begin to fire bullets at you. You need to fly over with your range and press Fire to lock onto the turret and begin shooting at it.

In addition to the turret on the far right side, there is also an upgrade hatch. You can lock onto it like you would an enemy to break it open and find an upgrade. In this level, it is an upgrade that will extend the angular range for locking onto targets. Keep in mind that you need to pick up the upgrade before killing all enemies, as the level will instantly win when that happens and you will not be able to pick up the upgrade.

## Mission #01 - Sneak attack
This level contains two turrets with one gun, as well as one turret with two guns that is able to fire multiple bullets at once. Ideally you would barge towards the turret with the two guns and destroy it first, as this will make it easier to pick up the upgrade in the level, then destroy the two remaining turrets.

Bottom right of the level contains an upgrade hatch with a range upgrade.

## Mission #02 - Just one dish
This level features a radar dish that will try to locate you. While it is in range of you, it will make surrounding turrets fire bullets at you more frequently. While it is optional to destroy the radar to complete the level, it becomes a useful strategy to do so for further levels.

There are three turrets in the level - the top one is a turret with three guns, the one to the right has two guns and the one to the left has a single gun. It can be most useful to destroy the top turret first to clear out the area and then destroy the right turret before going for the upgrade hatches.

At the top of the level there are two upgrade hatches. The one to the left contains a range upgrade, while the one to the right contains a blade upgrade. The blades on your helicopter act as your health, and this will either heal your blades if you are low on health, or increase the amount of blades you have.

## Mission #03 - Challenge 1
Once you start the level, you will see a single lone turret at the bottom of the level guarding an upgrade hatch. Destroy it and pick up the upgrade, which is a blade upgrade/heal.

Going up in the level you will see a huge barrage of bullets from four turrets with three guns each. The bullets will despawn right before they reach your spawn area, so try to dodge the bullets and go either to the left or the right into the larger area of the level.

The four turrets are all clustered at the center of the large area, so a good strategy is to circle around the turrets and lock onto them one by one to destroy them.

## Mission #04 - Laser land
This is the first level featuring lasers. They will move linearly across a line attempting to line up with you, and will shoot very dangerous lasers from its sides. The sides gradually turn red to give a warning of when the laser will fire. However, while the laser is firing, it will not move so you can use this to your advantage to dodge the lasers.

First off, go down in the level to find a secret area with an upgrade hatch. It contains an artillery upgrade, which will allow you to put an artillery strike on the map. It is a good idea to save this for a later level.

In addition to the two lasers in this level, there are four turrets on the sides. The lower turrets are not within the ranges of the lasers so they are easy to destroy, but the upper turrets are within the range of the lasers so you will need to be careful. Lock onto the turrets and then hug the walls to avoid the lasers.

There are three lasers - one moves horizontally while two moves vertically. It may be a good idea to charge at and lock onto the horizontal laser first, then fly away before the laser fires. Then it is easy to destroy the two vertical lasers from below as they will never be able to hurt you from that angle.

There is another upgrade hatch at the top of the level, but I'm not sure what it contains as it does not seem to do anything.

## Mission #05 - Supply raid
This level is quite large. Begin by taking out the turret that is right within your spawn area, and then proceed down to a secret area to the left with an upgrade hatch. It contains another artillery strike. Go back out of the secret area and proceed further down to destroy another turret. The rest of the level consists of three smaller rooms to the right, each containing turrets and upgrade hatches:

- The top room is guarded by a turret at the entrance, and contains another turret inside the room. There is an upgrade hatch containing a weapon upgrade.
- The middle room is guarded by a turret at the entrance, and contains two more turrets inside the room. There is an upgrade hatch containing a range upgrade.
- The bottom room is guarded by two turrets at the entrance, and contains three more turrets inside the room. Begin by taking out the two turrets at the entrance, retreating to dodge the bullets, and then go back in to destroy the three turrets inside. There is an upgrade hatch containing a health upgrade.

## Mission #06 - Challenge 2
This level is a more challenging version of Mission #03 - Challenge 1.

Go down to the bottom of the level to find a single turret guarding a upgrade hatch. Destroy it and open the upgrade hatch to find a health upgrade. Then make your way up to the larger top area of the level.

Compared to Challenge 1, there are significantly more turrets in this level. Wait until there is an opening in the pattern of bullets and then fly either to the left of the right. There are four small turrets with one gun by the walls which you ideally should destroy first, and then proceed to circle around the turrets in the center of the level to destroy them one by one. Ideally you should start with the big turrets with three guns, then the smaller turrets with one gun each.

## Mission #12 - Getting Dizzy
This one is **hard**, and described by Adam as the first \*REAL\* challenge in the game. For beating the game, he gives the following advice:

> Tackling it head-on without any powerups or artillery strikes can be difficult.
>
> 1\) If you can manage to get here with an artillery (there is one in level 10), just blast the rockets when you get close enough (1 wall in between).
>
> 2\) The head-on approach: Grab the first powerup without getting hit. This is a weapon upgrade. Then proceed as slowly as you can while avoiding the heat-seekers, taking out the radar on the way. The power-ups in there will help a lot too. If you can get good at dodging the missiles, you can make 2 or 3 passes to get the powerups / kill the radar. This is HARD.
>
