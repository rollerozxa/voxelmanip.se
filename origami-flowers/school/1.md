Growing a flower is fun and easy. To keep it growing, all you need to do is give your flower both sun and water. Below is the status box, flower's sun and water.

Your flower uses up some of its sun and water every time you refresh the status by opening another menu screen, or going back to the main flower display by pressing **back** on your phone.

(Figure: Table of flower information)

Your sun and water gets used up at a rate of 1hr for every \*real\* hour of time that passes. You will learn how to speed up growth in future lessons, but **remember that you need sun and water, and it constantly gradually gets used up.**

To increase your reserves of sun and water, open **Items** from the (menu) button on the main flower screen. Among many other items, that is where you will find **water and sun overdose**, much like the links displayed below. Go ahead and give it a try to see how your sun and water reserves increase.
