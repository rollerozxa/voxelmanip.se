**Offer bonus** helps you get **<u>more stars</u>**. **Offer bonus** can also be used to buy **checklists**... that's all. So why do we have an entire lesson dedicated to **offer bonus**? Becuase offer bonus can be the difference between getting **500\* stars**, or getting **150 MILLION stars** from an
offer.

So how does **offer bonus** work? Simply put, every time you earn an **offer bonus reward** from completing a **quiz, app download or star purchase**, you may get one or more **offer bonus rewards**. As long as the offer is worth at least **3 stars** (sometimes changes), you **will** get your **offer bonus reward**.

How many times can I get the **offer bonus reward**? That is calculated using 2 pieces of information. The first 1s the size of the **offer**. If the **offer** you complete is worth **between 3\* and 150\***, you will receive the offer bonus 1x. For every **150\*** the offer is worth, you will receive the **offer bonus reward <u>*again*</u>**.

**Example:** If you open up the **(Get STARS)** screen and complete an offer worth **340\***, you will get the **offer bonus reward** 2 times. Calculated like this: **340 / 150 = 2**.

**Another Example:** If you open up the (Buy STARS) screen and complete an offer worth **5000\* stars**, you will get the **offer bonus reward** 33 times. Calculated like this: **5000 / 150 = 33**.

Ok, so I know how many times I will get the **offer bonus reward**, but how many stars is that worth? Simple, it's worth your **offer bonus**. If you have an **offer bonus** of **100**, you
will get **100\* stars** for each and every **offer bonus reward**. If you have an offer bonus of **23,000**, you will receive **\*23,000 stars** for every **offer bonus reward**. The most you can receive from a **single offer bonus reward** is *currently* **150,000.00\* stars** (changes sometimes).

...ok, hang on... does that mean you just said that I can get up to **<u>150,000.00* stars</u>** from a **SINGLE** offer that only shows a value of **2\*** on the **(Get STARS)** screen?!?!? ... **<u>NO. You can get up to a maximum of 50000 x offer value for each reward, or 150,000.00*, whichever is smaller (<em>changes sometimes</em>).</u>**

...ok, now just hang on here... does that also mean that I can get up to **<u>75,000,000.00* BONUS STARS</u>** from a **SINGLE** purchase of **75 thousand** stars? ... **<u>YES.</u>**

That's AMAZING! But how can I build up my **offer bonus** to take advantage of this **incredible** bonus system? Your **offer bonus** is increased by 1 every time you complete an offer from the **flower school quizzes, (Get STARS), and (Buy STARS)** screens. If you receive the **offer bonus reward** multiple times, it will also increase your **offer bonus** by one for each reward received.

Is there any OTHER way I can increase my **offer bonus**? Of course! The fastest way to increase your **offer bonus**, is through the **Seeds draw instant win prizes**. Spend as many seeds as you can on the **seeds draw**, and the more you do, the better your chance of winning an increase to your personal **offer bonus**. It really is that easy. Also, sometimes the prizes in the **Global Compost Heap** include increases to the **offer bonus**, as well as the **stars draw instant win prizes**.
