In this lesson, we learn more about the **Items** screen, which is reached from the **(Spend stars on ITEMS)**, or **(Menu)** button on the flower display.

Displayed now in the stats box along with sun, and water, is **seeds**, **stars**, and **giga** fertilizer.

(Figure: Table of flower information)

**Seeds and stars** are the currency of flowers. **Seeds** are earned automatically all of the time, at a rate equal to your current **seed income** (displayed on items screen below). **Stars** are earned from the **(Get STARS)** screen, by downloading games, watching videos or from the **(Buy STARS)** screen.

**Giga fertilizer** is used up in real time, just like sun and water. While you have giga fertilizer on your flower, it adds additional multipliers to the growth rate.

Also available on the **Items** screen is **fertilizer**, and **super fertilizer**. Owning these items adds **even more** to your growth multipliers.
