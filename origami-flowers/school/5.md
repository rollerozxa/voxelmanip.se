In this lesson, we learn about the seeds and stars draws.

Both draws can be found by accessing the **(Menu)** from the main flower screen, or the **[shortcuts]** in chatterbox.

In the **Draw** screen, you can buy *tickets*. When you buy a *ticket*, you have a chance to win an **instant win prize**. The prizes are listed below the ticket purchasing choices.

You may buy as many draw tickets as you can afford. There is a limit to the number of entries you have in the draw, but you may still continue to purchase tickets to win the **instant win prizes**.

The big draw prize is increased with every ticket purchased by players until the draw ends. When the draw ends, one ticket is randomly chosen from all entries, and the lucky owner of that ticket wins the big draw prize. After the prize is awarded, a new draw is started, and the big prize is reset.

	Good job, you have a chance to win the next big stars draw! Remember: more tickets means a better chance of winning!
