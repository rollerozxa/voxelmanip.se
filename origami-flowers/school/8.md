The **Stars Exchange** screen can be accessed from the main screen **(Menu)** or chatterbox **[shortcuts]**.

The **Stars Exchange** is a live market for players to buy and sell stars with each other, using seeds as the currency. All of the buy and sell orders are created by other players.

Displayed on the **Stars Exchange** screen is the *highest bid* price currently in the system, that a player is walling to *pay** for another player's stars. If you sell stars immediately, this is the highest price that you will get if you put some stars up for sale.

Also displayed on the **Stars Exchange** screen is the *lowest offer* price currently in the system, that a player is willing to *sell* their own stars for. If you buy stars immediately, this is the lowest price you will have to pay to purchase some stars.

Because each open buy and sell order has a limited quantity of stars, the price paid changes depending on how many stars you buy or sell on the market. The quantity available for the currently highest bid and sale are displayed, so that you can know how many stars can be executed at that displayed price.

To complete a transaction on the **Stars Exchange**, simply enter the quantity and price you desire for your purchase or sale of stars. If you choose a price that is too high or too low to be executed immediately, the order will stay open **(and hold your seeds / stars)** until you cancel it, or another player matches it. **You must already hold enough seeds to make the purchase, or enough stars to make the sale**, or the order will not be entered into the exchange.

If you wish to buy or sell a large quantity of stars, simply enter the desired quantity, and the maximum or minimum price you are willing to accept. Purchases and sales are executed in a specific order.

When you **purchase** stars, you are given the *lowest* price available first, followed by the next *cheapest* open price *if the first order was filled*, etc. This ensures that you always pay the *best (lowest)* price possible when buying lots of stars.

When you **sell** stars, you are given the highest price available first, followed by the next highest open price if the first order was filled, best (highest) price possible when selling lots of stars.

Displayed below the buy / sell options, is a brief recent history of completed buy / sell orders on the exchange. This can be helpful when determining the price you want to target. If you are asking too high of a price when selling, or too low of a price when purchasing, the order may stay on the exchange for a very long time. If the exchange never reaches your target price, the order will stay open forever, unless you **cancel** it. When you **cancel** an open order, your seeds and/or stars are returned.
