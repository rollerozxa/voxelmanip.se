Like other important screens in the game, the **Global Compost Heap** can be accessed from main screen **(Menu)** or chatterbox **[shortcuts]**.

The **Heap** is a community driven reward game. There are many different heap **prize** packages. Each **prize** package has a **goal** that to be reached. To reach this **goal**, players *throw seeds* on the **heap**. Once the **goal** total is reached, prizes are awarded.

Heap **prizes** are awarded in several ways. It is even possible to receive the heap **prize** multiple times by being one of the players to be a *top contributor*. If you achieve one of these goals, you will be rewarded...

- **Be a top contributor.** The number of top contributors sometimes changes, it is displayed on the heap screen.
- Be the player that *'tips'* the heap by contributing the last required seeds to reach the heap goal.
- Be a *friend* of the player that *'tips'* the heap, ***and also contribute at least a little to the heap***

When you donate enough seeds to put yourself into the **top contributors** of the current heap, and that *bumps* another player out of the **top contributors**, you get an **instant bonus prize!** If you get *bumped* out of the top contributors, you will get a message, so be on the lookout and make sure to get back in to the **top contributors!**

Players that contribute the most to the heap **(top 3)** and tipping, also receive bonus **PGM**. **PGM** is explained in a later lesson, but know it is very good to have, and worth aiming for.

Great! With these seeds on the heap, you have a chance to win the heap prize!
