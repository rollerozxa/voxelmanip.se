The current **checklist** tasks are displayed at the bottom of the **flower selection** screen, or any **menu**.

Completing all of the tasks in a **checklist**, rewards you with a free **time jump** that does **not** deplete your flower resources. This works similar to the Time jump, but does **not deplete resources**, and does **not utilize PGM**.

The amount of growing you get from the reward jump is still limited by the amount of resources you hold on your flower. So if you only have **15hr** of resources, and get a **100hr** checklist reward: it’s only going to grow for **15hr** worth of time. It does **not** however **15hr** of resources on your flower after.

The **checklist reward jump** uses the **galaxy bonus warp and giga multipliers**. This that when you get the reward, it will have a greatly increased **growth multiplier** if you have **giga fertilizer**. It also means that the **warp speed** will be much faster than normal. The multipliers are determined by the current galaxy upgrades and can be seen in the **chatterbox** by typing **/g** into a chat message.

At the top of any menu screen you will be presented with several options for receiving your **checklist reward**...

- **<u>Do not override resource limit. (no additional cost)</u>**

	This is the normal reward, that limits the growing time to the amount of resources you are holding on your flower.

- **<u>Override resources limit. (cost: pgm)</u>**

	This allows you to jump as if you had more resources than required to cover the entire jump reward time. So even if you only have 1hr of resources, a 1000hr jump reward will still do 1000hr of growing!

- **<u>10x checklists (cost: pgm + offer bonus)</u>**

	This is the same as doing 10 normal jump rewards in a row. There is a cost to getting multiple rewards, so make sure you have enough.

- **<u>10x override checklists (cost: pgm + offer bonus)</u>**

	This 1s the same as doing 10 reward jumps with the *override* option. Again, there are costs to do this.

- **<u>100x checklists (cost: pgm + offer bonus)</u>**

	This is the same as doing 100 normal jump rewards in a row. There is a cost to getting multiple rewards, so make sure you have enough.

- **<u>100x override checklists (cost: pgm + offer bonus)</u>**

	This 1s the same as doing 100 reward jumps with the *override* option. Again, there are costs to do this.

Checklists can be **bought** using **offer bonus** if you don't want to complete all of the tasks. However, when completing a checklist properly, you gain **bonus PGM!** So spend the time to complete the tasks. All of the tasks help your flower in some way, so it's a good idea to complete **at least one** every day! If you buy or complete 750 checklists, it will cost additional **PGM** when purchasing.
