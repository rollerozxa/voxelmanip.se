In this lesson, we learn how to make the flower grow in fast-forward using **warp**.

Remember that sun, water and giga are used up in real time. If you have 2 hours of sun, water and giga, then open your flower 1.5 hours later: all 3 will be down to 0.5hr.

(Figure: Table of flower information)

Now we have added **warp** to the status box. When our flower has **warp**, it will **SPEED UP TIME**. This makes your flower grow as if it is being put on fast-forward. All of your resources will be used up at the warp rate, but your flower will also grow faster. You must be careful to **always** have more sun, and water than you have warp. That will ensure that warping does not use up all of your basic resources.

If you buy 1 hour of warp, It will fast-forward your flower until 1hr of flower time has passed, thus using up 1hr of flower resources.

**Example:** If you have 10hr of sun and water, but only buy 2 hours of warp: when the warp runs out, you will still have 8hr of sun and water.

**Good job, your flower is warping!** Open up the main flower screen to watch your flower grow super fast!
