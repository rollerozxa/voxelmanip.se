In this lesson, we learn a handful of great strategies that can kickstart your flower growing experience.

1. **Add Friends.** In an earlier lesson you were rewarded with a pile of empty friend slots. Fill them up! You get **\*FREE STUFF\*** every time a friend does any of these...
	* Tips a heap
	* Plays and earns seeds
	* Downloads apps and earns stars
	* Buys bulk resources for their flower
	* Earns stars from finishing lessons in flower school
2. **Talk to players in chat.** Our **(Chatterbox)** is monitored by moderators to keep everything friendly. Angry and offensive players are actively removed from the conversation. Chat is the best way to discuss strategies with other players. Our long time veterans are always happy to help out, and offer tips.
3. **Play every day.** Your seed income is only rewarded in 24hr chunks at a time. If you wait 2 days before opening your flower, you will only eam 24hr of seed income upon opening it. Playing several times every day makes it easy to keep track of your inbox messages as well. In the **(Inbox)** screen, you can turn off the option to permanently save "Friend offer completed" messages if you find your inbox too cluttered.
4. **Read the <span class="red">(\* Galaxy Special: \*)</span> on the flower choice screen at the top.** There is always an active galaxy special. Sometimes it is an increase in prizes from the draws, sometimes it's a discount on resources or transfers, or any number of possibilities. Always give it a glance on the way into the game, to see if you can take advantage!
5. **Throw a few seeds on the heap.** Details on the **(Global Compost Heap)** are explained in the next lesson, but it doesn't hurt to start now. At least contribute the minimum amount, and on many heaps that's enough to win the pnize!
