**Time Jump** on the **(Items)** screen is used to jump your flower ahead in time when you want to use of a large amount of your resources. you do **<u>NOT</u>** get seed income for buying time jump on the items screen.

This jump will use the **current** giga multiplier and warp speed, and is only available during galaxy bonus. **Galaxy bonus** time is when the giga and warp values are greatly increased. To determine how much time you can jump use this simple formula...

**time_to_jump = (smallest_resource_amount / warp_speed)**

So if you only have **30,000hr** of giga fertilizer as your lowest resource, and the warp speed 1s **5000x**, that means you only need to jump for **(30,000 / 5,000) = 6hr** of time to use your resources.

Some **important points** about buying time jump from the items screen:

- Time jump uses your **PGM** to increase the growth multiplier for your jump. So if the growth multiplier is currently **500x**, and you are holding **1000 PGM**, the total growth multiplier will be **1500x** for your jump!
- When you **time jump**, your **PGM** is reduced by **1/2**.
- When you **time jump**, if the total amount of height you gain 1s over **1 billion cm**, 1t will be multiplied by the cusrently displayed amount.
