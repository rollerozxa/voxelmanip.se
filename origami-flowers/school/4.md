In this lesson, we learn about the value of our flower friends.

Why are friends important in the flower games? Because when you earn seeds from your seed income, your friends get free seeds. These are \*NOT\* deducted from you, they are 100% **BONUS / FREE / EXTRA** on top of your income. So it helps every one of your friends every time you play! When you earn stars from the **(Get STARS)** screen, your friends also earn **FREE** stars. In the flower games, almost everything you do helps out others.

Below is what the **(Friends)** screen looks like. The **(Friends)** screen is accessed from the main menu or shortcuts in **(Chatterbox)**. From the **(Friends)** screen, you can send messages to players on your friend list, or remove players from your list. To reply to friend messages, you need to access the **(Inbox)** screen from the menu or chat shortcuts.

<strong class="red">\*IMPORTANT\*</strong> **(Chatterbox)** is a moderated family friendly chat room. Bad behaviour will result in losing chat privileges or even player-2-player messages and having your name censored. Please be friendly, and others will be friendly with you.

The easiest way to add friends is from the **(Chatterbox)**. To access the **(Chatterbox)** go back to the main screen, and touch the icon labelled **(Chatterbox)**. Inside the **(Chatterbox)** you can introduce yourself, or even just lurk. (lurking is boring, say hi!). When you see a player with a <span class="blue-underline">blue underlined name</span>, that means they are actively looking for a new friend! Touch the <span class="blue-underline">blue underlined name</span> and will instantly become flower friends.

When you post in **(Chatterbox)**, you can make yourself available for new friends by choosing to make yourself **CLICKABLE** in the dropdown box above where you type. Or you can fill as many empty friend spaces as you have available by choosing the **FRIEND FINDER** option.

<strong class="red">(Helpful Suggestion)</strong>... You should fill your friend list. You still have empty friend slots. Open the **(Chatterbox)** from the main screen, and add more friends!
