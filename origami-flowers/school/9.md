The **Transfer Between Flowers** screen can be accessed from the main screen **(Menu)** or chatterbox **[shortcuts]**.

To be able to transfer between flowers, you need to **GROW MULTIPLE FLOWERS!** Make sure you have started growing each flower from the **Choose your flower** screen. Having multiple flowers is helpful, because you can get free resources when you win heap prizes, or when you or your friends buy bulk resources. Those resources can then be transferred to your favorite flower.

The **Transfer Between Flowers** screen allows you to move resources from other flowers, into the flower you currently have open. This can be helpful for many reasons...

- Transfer **time** to gain seed income from **closed** flowers, and jump ahead growing on your currently **open** flower
- Transfer **height** to consolidate height and gain rank on your main flower
- Transfer **sun**, **water**, **giga fertilizer** to keep your open flower well stocked
- Transfer **warp** away from a closed flower if you don't want it to warp next time you open it
- Transfer **growth rate** from your other flowers, to make sure your main flower is growing as fast as possible
