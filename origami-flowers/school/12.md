The **Galaxy** serves 2 purposes in the flower games.

1. To give players a way to earn **bonus stars** during the **Galactic Construction** phase
2. To give players a way to grow with increased **growth multipliers** and **warp speed** for a short period known as the **Galactic Bonus** phase

The **Galaxy** has 2 phases. Each galaxy starts in the **construction** phase. The construction starts at **0%**, and increases every time players earn stars from **flower school, (Get STARS), and (Buy STARS)** screens. During the **construction** phase of the galaxy, players can earn bonus stars from both the **galactic speedup** and from **construction bonuses** called **moons, suns, solar systems, and spiral arms**.

To estimate how many stars you can earn from the **galactic construction and speedup** bonuses, try these simple rules of thumb...

(Figure: Galactic construction status)

- Type /g into chatterbox, to see the current **speedup** and **construction information**
- The speedup is **directly multiplied** by the stars you earn to give you the **speedup bonus**. So if the speedup is **50%**, and you earn **100\*** stars from a flower school quiz, you will receive an additional **100 x 50% = 50\*** stars as a speedup bonus. if the speedup is **1200%**, you would earn **1200% x 100\*** = 1200* stars as a **speedup bonus**.
- Every time a player earns stars from an **offer** (get stars, flower school quiz, buy stars), the speedup is increased. The more stars the **offer** is worth, the more it will increase the **galaxy speed**.
- To estimate the stars you may earn from the **galactic construction** bonus, first determine the **speedup bonus** you will earn, then look at the **stars remaining** to complete each of the different construction bonuses. (moon, sun, etc.) For example, if the galaxy message says that there are **500\*** until a **sun bonus**, you need to earn **500\*** stars from the combination of **speedup bonus and offer value** from an **offer** (quiz, get / buy stars) to get that bonus.
- Timing is tricky to get the construction bonuses, so it's never a guarantee that you will get one that you are trying to earn using a small offer.

Once the **galactic construction** reaches **100%**, the **Galactic bonus** phase begins. This generally lasts for **48hours**, and the time remaining can be seen by typing **/g** into **(Chatterbox)**. During the **Galactic bonus** phase, you do not receive speedup or construction bonuses for stars earned.

During the **Galactic bonus** phase, the **growth multipliers** and **warp speed** are greatly increased. This makes 1t a great time for growing your flower. Increase your supplies during the **construction** phase, so that you can grow as much as possible using the **higher growth multipliers** expenienced in the **galactic bonus** phase.

The value of the **giga fertilizer growth multiplier** and the **warp speed** during **galactic bonus** is determined by the **galaxy upgrades**. The galaxy is **upgraded** by making donations of **seeds** on the menu screens.

The **Galactic Hero** is the player that spends a large amount of their saved up **PGM** to carry over the increased galaxy upgrades from the previous galaxy, to the next. Because the cost **is very high**, the **Galactic Hero** receives several rewards during the time he/she is the hero...

- A small amount of seeds or stars from every purchase of tickets in the **seeds and stars draws**
- A small amount of seeds from donations to the **Global Compost Heap**
- A small amount of stars from purchases of **bulk** resources
* A small amount of seeds from every time a player passes the potato and receives PGM

To become the **Galactic Hero**, you must be first to pay the **PGM cost** to carry forward the galactic upgrades. This option is displayed at the **top** of every menu page until a player pays for it. After a hero has been determined, the option is replaced by the message...

**We have a GALACTIC HERO!**
